Talk to it like you'd talk to a maker.

Hold the button and describe what you want — “a hexagonal planter with drainage holes,” “a dragon curled around a d20,” “a name tag that says ARLO in rounded letters.” The model listens, sculpts a watertight mesh, and shows you the result before a single gram of filament moves.

Engineer mode

Real parts, from real dimensions.

Need a bracket, a mount, a clip, a spacer? Give Conjure the measurements and it drives parametric CAD — not a fuzzy guess. Every part is generated from exact constraints, checked for wall thickness and load, and flagged if the geometry can't take the stress you described.
